College Help Portal

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the college working hours?
The college generally operates from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on working days.
How can I access the college library?
Students can access the library using their valid college identity card during library hours.
Where can I check my examination timetable?
The examination timetable is available on the college notice board and official student portal.
How can I contact the department?
Students can contact the department office or use the official department email address.
How can I apply for a leave?
Students can submit a leave application to their class teacher or through the college portal if available.
